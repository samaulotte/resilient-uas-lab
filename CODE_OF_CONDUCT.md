# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Resilient UAS Lab is built by and for engineers who care about making autonomous systems
safer. We want everyone who takes part, whether by reporting a bug, proposing a scenario,
reviewing a pull request or asking a question, to be treated with respect and to be able
to contribute without fear of harassment or discrimination.

This code applies in every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions, review comments, and any other channel used to work on the project. It also
applies when an individual represents the project in public.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith and ask before concluding.
- Give credit, acknowledge mistakes and correct them.
- Respect differing levels of experience; explain rather than dismiss.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its defensive engineering purpose.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination or demeaning remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Using project channels to pursue capabilities the project explicitly excludes
  (interference, spoofing or attack techniques against real systems), or to solicit
  such material from others.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this code, contact the project
maintainers privately. Maintainers are listed as members of the repository on GitHub and
can be reached through the contact details on their profiles. If the report concerns a
maintainer, contact a different maintainer, or use GitHub's own reporting tools.

Reports are handled confidentially. We will acknowledge a report, look into it, and tell
the reporter what we decided. We do not disclose the identity of a reporter without their
consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including editing or removing
contributions, issuing a warning, or temporarily or permanently excluding a participant
from project spaces. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may
be removed from the maintainer group.
